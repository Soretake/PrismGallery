Prism Gallery is a fast, private gallery for your photos and videos. It works entirely offline: the app has no internet permission, no ads, no tracking and no account.

It is made for custom ROMs that ship without a usable gallery, for phones whose only gallery is a cloud photo app, and for anyone who wants a smooth, good-looking gallery.

<b>Features</b>
<ul>
<li>Four design styles with their own layouts and animations, inspired by iOS (Liquid Glass), Samsung One UI, Xiaomi HyperOS and Google Gemini</li>
<li>Photos, videos and GIFs from internal storage and the SD card</li>
<li>Albums: create, rename, hide, sub-albums, move media between albums, move whole albums between internal storage and SD card</li>
<li>Album groups, "Other albums", grid or list view, home-screen shortcuts</li>
<li>Favorites, trash and a private album (PIN, fingerprint or screen lock)</li>
<li>Timeline by day, month or year; search by name, album, place, date or type; Places and "On this day", all offline</li>
<li>Deep zoom, readable even on huge photos and very long scroll screenshots</li>
<li>Slideshow, motion photos, HDR photos, video speed, loop and mute</li>
<li>Editor (saved as a copy): crop, rotate, mirror, adjustments, filters, drawing, text, watermark; videos: trim and make smaller</li>
<li>Edit date, time and location; share without location, as JPEG or smaller</li>
<li>Collages, GIFs, PDFs and printing</li>
<li>Clean up duplicates, similar photos, old screenshots, large videos and RAW files</li>
<li>Slideshow wallpaper, live wallpaper and photo widget</li>
<li>Storage overview for internal storage and SD card</li>
<li>Open photos and videos from other apps ("Open with")</li>
<li>English and German</li>
<li>Adjustable graphics quality for older or low-memory devices</li>
</ul>

Requires Android 10 or newer.

Prism Gallery is an independent project and is not affiliated with Apple, Samsung, Xiaomi or Google. Their names only describe the visual style each design is inspired by.
